# Privacy Boundary

Safety evaluation must not silently become surveillance.

## Defaults
- local-first state
- no telemetry required by the protocol
- no central location history
- no account required by the reference core
- bounded evidence retention
- explicit clearing of retained evidence in product adapters
- no secret material in receipts

## Claim ceiling
SafetyProtocol can support privacy-preserving connectivity. It does not establish absolute anonymity. Access points, carriers, operating systems, tunnel endpoints and destinations may each observe different metadata.

## Adapter rule
A platform permission that exposes location-adjacent or device-identifying information must be justified by a specific adapter capability. Availability of data does not create authority to retain or transmit it.
